Where can visitors park in Stockholm?

You can park on most streets where there is no parking ban and no cleaning day ("städdag"). Pay by the hour via the sign's zone, or use a parking app. ParkSpot shows — on a map — exactly where you may stand right now, the price, and which streets are cleaned tomorrow.

What does parking cost? (Tariff zones 1–5)

Street parking price depends on the zone — most expensive in the centre, cheapest in the outer areas:

Motorcycles have a lower tariff. Evenings and weekends are often free outside the charging hours.

⚠️ Watch out: street cleaning days

Each street has a weekly cleaning day when parking is forbidden — park there and you risk a fine and towing. Signs are in Swedish ("Servicedag" / day + time). ParkSpot shows tomorrow's cleaning streets on the map, so you can avoid them.

Parking overnight

On many streets it is legal (and often free at night) to park until the next morning — as long as the street is not cleaned the next day. ParkSpot's "Over natten" (overnight) mode highlights safe streets near you.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I park in central Stockholm as a tourist?

On legal street spots (pay by zone) or in a parking garage. ParkSpot shows where you may stand right now, the price, and the nearest garage.

How much is parking in Stockholm?

From 5 SEK/hour (zone 5, outer) to 55 SEK/hour (zone 1, city centre). Evenings and weekends are often free.

What is a "städdag" / cleaning day?

A weekly day when a street is cleaned and parking is banned. Parking on a cleaning day risks a fine and towing — ParkSpot shows tomorrow’s cleaning streets.

Can I park overnight in Stockholm?

Yes, on many streets and often free at night — as long as the street is not cleaned the next morning. ParkSpot highlights safe overnight streets.

Is ParkSpot free?

Yes, free and no login. It is based on the City of Stockholm open data.
